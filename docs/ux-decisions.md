# TrustPilot AI — UX Design Decisions & Visual Language

## 1. Visual Language & Emotional Resonance
The interface is designed with a **calm, trustworthy, modern fintech aesthetic**:
- **Palette:** Slate-900 / Deep Navy as structural anchors, Slate-50 for clean neutral background, Brand Sky/Blue for active actions, Emerald for trusted passes, Amber for attention/remediation, and Rose for critical risk.
- **Typography:** Inter for clarity and legibility; JetBrains Mono for hashes, identifiers, and OCR extracted fields.
- **Zero "Generic AI" Clutter:** Avoided oversized floating shapes, purple gradients, excessive glassmorphism, or cartoon illustrations. The product communicates operational security and rigorous engineering.

---

## 2. Progressive Disclosure in KYC
- Step-by-step guidance avoids cognitive overload.
- In-place optical quality checks provide immediate, human-friendly remediation (e.g. *"This photo is blurry. Please hold steady in bright lighting."*) rather than technical `OCR_500` errors.

---

## 3. High-Density Analyst Workspace
- Designed for speed: two-column layout keeps case timeline, signal decomposition, and document extraction simultaneously visible.
- Instant access to AI Copilot drawer for fast querying without losing context.
